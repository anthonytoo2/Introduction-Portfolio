Chaminade College School

Chaminade was the place that I grew the most. At Chaminade, I not only achieved incredible grades - top of many of my classes - but I was also an active member of the community. From the start of high school, I was a part of the Robotics club, starting off as a member, and working my way to become the Mechanical lead (who oversees the construction of the bot) as well as becoming the Team Captain of 2634. It was thanks to the teaching staff at Chaminade that I was able to learn so many new things, become proficient at them, becoming the school's second Schulich Leader.
